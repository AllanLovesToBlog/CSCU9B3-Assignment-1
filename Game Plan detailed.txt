1. My topic (approved by lec)
    The database schema will be data from healthcare insurance claims
    and will be used to train a fraud detection AI model. The schema 
    will contain 5 tables namely claims_data (containing user claims 
    used for insurance), ailment_medicine (contains drug ID and the 
    ailment it cures, dosage, drug price etc), providers (containing
    list of licensed medical providers), members (contains personal 
    customer data) and ICD_10_ailment_description (contains the official 
    WHO list of ailments and their ICD 10 codes)

2. Tables schema
    1. claims_data - (claim_id[PK], members_id[FK], provider_id[FK], icd_10_code[FK],
                      service_date, claim_type, billed_amount, paid_amount, fraud_score)
    2. members - (member_id[PK], member_name, gender, age)
    3. providers - (provider_id[PK], provider_name, provider_status)
    4. ICD_10_ailment_description - (icd_10_code[PK], ailment_name, description)
    5. ailment_medicine - (drug_id[PK], icd_10_code[FK], drug_name, dosage, price)
